autoclips — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

autoclips is a personal tool operated by its owner to publish short videos to the owner's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • Access tokens for the owner's own social accounts, granted through each platform's official login.
  • Basic profile information (account name, ID) and statistics for videos the tool published (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • Video files the owner chose to publish.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and show their performance to the owner. It is stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Video files are deleted from the computer within 30 days of publishing. Tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected. Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's account settings.
